# FIIU Fest in NODAL

## Brief and sources

Implement the September 28 request and Flavia's FIIU x NODAL board within the existing plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript and Node architecture. Source form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1zvj20L8MqH84WHh0XxsgGfEE1qhEF-X4RgO3QrPP8fk/edit. Event: https://fiiu.sistemaurbano.org/. Board: https://app.clickup.com/4209584/whiteboards/40exg-29877.

The public landing page is linkable before login. Registration uses the existing NODAL account and returns to the festival after authentication. One editable registration per account records five general conference blocks (October 21 morning/evening, October 22 morning/evening, October 23 morning). October 20 is an optional reviewed laboratory application for public officials, never automatic admission. Nine workshops and five routes have individual dates and Google Forms links; NODAL records interest separately from conference registration. Completing the facilitator's Google Form remains necessary, and neither interest nor a link click proves external registration or attendance. A person can register interest without selecting a conference. The closing party remains separately registered. Dates are local to Lima. Programme content may change; display the official programme link.

## Architecture and decisions

Dedicated festival domain, repository and API modules support both SQLite and Supabase. New tables hold private registrations, administrator-confirmed attendance, configurable links/open status, and published news/material links. New Supabase tables have RLS and server-only grants; ownership, administrator permission, same-origin protection, limits and optimistic versions are enforced at the application boundary. Account export and deletion include festival records. Registrations cannot grant roles, laboratory acceptance or badges. CSV exports neutralize spreadsheet formulas.

The form is inspiration: correct its outdated Cusco motivation option to a location-neutral choice. Account email is authoritative. Per the September 29 feedback, identity, age, gender, accessibility, motivation and previous-attendance answers are required. An explicit no-support answer and prefer-not-to-say gender remain valid; Other text is required only when Other is selected. These answers stay private, are never added to public profiles, and are not saved in browser storage. Registration remains subject to venue capacity. Historical incomplete records stay readable, exportable and cancellable; the attendee must complete missing fields on their next save.

The navigation reflects Programa / Comunidad / Recursos / Conocimiento / Mi panel. Existing community and catalog capabilities remain available. A community hub highlights FIIU, urban meetups and events; knowledge connects to existing courses and resources. Published festival news appear on the dashboard, along with the member's registration and earned badges. Only administrator-confirmed attendance earns a badge. News/resources use real published content and honest empty states.

Design follows NODAL's Montserrat typography and green identity: forest #3d5c38, leaf #59bc53, pale green #dcebd6, white #ffffff, ink #20291e. A broad festival title and compact date/venue panel lead into a readable day-by-day programme. Registration uses grouped fieldsets with a visible saved state; the public-official branch stays next to the programme choices. All controls work on mobile, with keyboard focus and English/Spanish/Portuguese interface text.

## October 1 second round

Scope decisions for this round:
- No confirmation email; that is a later round.
- No new role. Flavia, the owner and the other administrators publish and run check-in as `app_role = 'admin'`. Production grants are made by the lead, never by code.
- No venue or time overrides.
- No pinned flag on festival news, and no festival-updates list on the community hub.

**QR check-in**
- Only the six NODAL blocks can be checked in: `day0-lab`, `day1-am`, `day1-pm`, `day2-am`, `day2-pm` and `day3-am`. Workshops and routes stay with their facilitators. The server answers them with 400 `invalid_activity`, and no screen lists them.
- `/fiiu-qr.html?a=<block>` (administrators only) shows a rotating QR of `/fiiu-checkin.html?a=<block>&c=<code>`, the address to type, a 6-character code and a live count.
  - Codes are stateless HMACs. A new one starts every 60 s, and the current code plus the four before it are accepted, so a photo of the screen stops working within about 5 minutes.
  - The secret is `FIIU_CHECKIN_SECRET`, or else derived from the Supabase server key.
  - The screen follows the server clock (`serverTime`, `rotatesAt`), never the device's.
  - The QR is drawn as one SVG path from server-packed bits. The encoder is `server/qr.js`, which has no dependencies: byte mode, level M, versions 1–10. CoreImage, Vision and Edge's BarcodeDetector decoded all 128 test codes back exactly.
- `POST /api/fiiu/checkin` needs a signed-in, same-origin attendee and has its own limit of 20 requests per minute per account.
  - It accepts a scan only inside the block's window, from 30 minutes before the start to 30 minutes after the end, Lima time (fixed −05:00). The laboratory has no time yet, so its window is the whole of 20 October in Lima.
  - The block must be in the attendee's registration; the laboratory needs an accepted application.
  - Otherwise the page says why and, while registration is open, links to `fiiu.html#registration` so the person can add the block and scan again. The laboratory cannot be added there, so a laboratory refusal says that only accepted applications check in and points to the registration desk.
  - A repeat scan with a valid screen code keeps the first confirmation and answers `already_checked_in`, even after the window has closed, the block has left the plan or an edited laboratory application is back under review.
  - A refusal outside the window carries the server's `serverTime`, so the page says "not open yet" or "closed" from the server clock, never the phone's.
  - Rows record `method` `'qr'` or `'staff'`. Team confirmation in the participant detail still works.
- A signed-out scan opens the check-in page itself, which says "Sign in to check in" for that block. Its sign-in link returns with both `a` and `c`, because `safeNext` keeps the query string.
- Reloading the page after a check-in (the address keeps only `a`) shows the confirmation again from the person's registration, not an incomplete-link error. Signed out, a reload (or Back from the sign-in page) asks for sign-in and returns to the same block; signed in with no confirmation, it asks for the screen code.
- The organiser dashboard has a Check-in section listing the six blocks with their windows, live counts and screen links. The overview adds checked-in people (with how many of them used the QR at least once, `qrPeople`), hours confirmed and a day ledger. The participant detail shows QR or Team per row and the person's certificate hours.

**Hours**
- Only conference blocks count: 240 minutes for a morning block, 120 for an evening block.
- The laboratory counts for attendance and badges but adds 0 h until its length is known.
- One scan counts as the full block. A person's total is rounded half up to whole hours.
- The participant CSV adds 11 columns: attended blocks and days, minutes, certificate hours, methods, and one Lima check-in time per block.

**Organiser-only pages**
- `/fiiu-admin.html` and `/fiiu-qr.html` send signed-out visitors to sign-in.
- A signed-in member gets `organisers-only.html` with status 403. That page carries no organiser data and no organiser script.
- `/admin.html` and `/teaching.html` keep their JSON 403.
- All three new pages are server-only (`SERVER_PAGES`), `no-store` and `noindex`.

**NODAL news**
- `/admin.html` became the trilingual Publishing desk: NODAL news, opportunities and catalog (with a "Before publishing" checklist that mirrors the server's EN/ES/PT plus verified-source gate), links to festival news and courses, and the interest queue.
- News is stored in the new `nodal_news` table on both backends, behind `GET /api/news` (public) and `/api/admin/news` (administrators). Writes use optimistic versions, creates can be retried safely with client ids, and pages use keyset cursors.
- `web/scripts/news-feed.js` shows the posts on the console (3) and on community.html (10, with "More news"). It renders with textContent only and stays hidden when there is nothing to show.
- Administrators see a "Publishing" link in the console's Tools rail.
