Membresía Fundador 2027

Las ciudades cambian cuando nos conectamos

Súmate a Nodal y sé parte desde el comienzo.

Si te apasionan las ciudades y quieres aportar a transformarlas, aquí puedes encontrar personas con quienes compartir ideas, aprender y dar el siguiente paso.

Nodal es una comunidad para conectar, colaborar y transformar nuestras ciudades desde el Urbanismo Colaborativo. Con tu Membresía Fundador accedes a conocimientos, herramientas y oportunidades para impulsar tu trabajo, y formas parte de la comunidad que está construyendo este espacio desde el inicio.

Un año para conectar, aprender y hacer crecer tus ideas

  • Conecta con personas que comparten tus intereses.

    Amplía tu red, participa en espacios de encuentro y descubre posibles colaboraciones a través de conexiones e introducciones curadas.

  • Encuentra oportunidades para dar el siguiente paso.

    Explora convocatorias, empleos, fondos y otras oportunidades seleccionadas para la comunidad.

  • Sigue aprendiendo.

    Tu membresía incluye un curso y una masterclass durante 2027, además de acceso a la biblioteca y los recursos de Nodal. También tienes 20% de descuento en cursos y masterclasses adicionales, no acumulable con otras promociones o tarifas especiales; se aplica la tarifa más favorable.

  • Haz visible lo que haces.

    Comparte tu experiencia, proyectos y contribuciones con un perfil ampliado y oportunidades de difusión vinculadas a tu participación.

Entra antes y ayúdanos a darle forma a Nodal

Al sumarte durante 2026, accedes a la plataforma desde la aprobación de tu primer pago, sin costo adicional hasta el 31 de diciembre.

Tus ideas y tu experiencia contribuirán a darle forma a Nodal para que responda mejor a las necesidades de su comunidad.

Founder Membership 2027

Cities change when we connect

Join Nodal and be part of it from the very beginning.

If you are passionate about cities and want to help transform them, here you can find people to share ideas with, learn with and take the next step.

Nodal is a community for connecting, collaborating and transforming our cities through Collaborative Urbanism. With your Founder Membership you get knowledge, tools and opportunities to drive your work forward, and you become part of the community that is building this space from the start.

A year to connect, learn and grow your ideas

  • Connect with people who share your interests.

    Widen your network, take part in gatherings and discover possible collaborations through curated connections and introductions.

  • Find opportunities to take the next step.

    Explore open calls, jobs, funding and other opportunities selected for the community.

  • Keep learning.

    Your membership includes one course and one masterclass during 2027, as well as access to the Nodal library and resources. You also get 20% off additional courses and masterclasses, not combinable with other promotions or special rates; the most favourable rate applies.

  • Make what you do visible.

    Share your experience, projects and contributions with an expanded profile and opportunities to showcase your work linked to your participation.

Come in early and help us shape Nodal

When you join during 2026, you can use the platform from the approval of your first payment, at no extra cost, until 31 December.

This early access is also an invitation to build together: explore the platform, try its tools and tell us what you find useful, what you would improve and what you would like to find. Your ideas and your experience will help shape Nodal so that it better meets the needs of its community.

Your place in Nodal's story

Founder

Being a Founder means being there from the start and contributing to what comes next.

When you join, you will receive the Founder badge, a mark that will stay on your profile in recognition of having been part of the founding community. Even if you later decide not to renew your membership, you will keep this distinction for as long as you keep your Nodal account.

The badge recognises your history in the community. The paid benefits and the renewal discount depend on keeping your membership active under its conditions.

A whole year, from the equivalent of S/41.58 a month

The Founder Membership 2027 is annual, and you can pay for it in one or two parts:

OptionHow do you pay?Monthly equivalent*
Single payment: S/499One payment when you sign up.S/41.58 a month
Split payment: S/550 in totalS/275 when you sign up and S/275 automatically two calendar months after your first payment is approved.S/45.83 a month

The same benefits with either option. All prices include IGV.

The monthly equivalent is the total price divided by the 12 months of membership, rounded to two decimal places. It is a reference for the cost: no monthly charges are made.

Start exploring today. Enjoy your membership throughout 2027.

Your annual membership runs from 1 January to 31 December 2027, in addition to the early access you get when you join in 2026.

The included course and masterclass are unlocked from your first payment: you do not need to complete the second one to access them. The activities will take place during 2027, subject to scheduling and available places, and you must keep your membership up to date.

Bring your ideas, your experience and your eagerness to collaborate. Let's build new possibilities for our cities together.

I want to be part of Nodal

Offer available to those who sign up and have their first payment approved by 31 December 2026.

Your membership renews automatically on 1 January 2028, unless you ask for it not to be renewed before that date. As long as you keep your Founder status active, you will get 5% off the regular rate then in force for your payment option. Before the renewal we will let you know the price and the payment dates.
