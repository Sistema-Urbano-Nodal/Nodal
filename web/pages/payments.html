Annual membership 2027

Founder Membership

Be part of the community building a new way to connect, collaborate and transform our cities through Collaborative Urbanism.

Choose how to pay

Both options include the same benefits. All prices include IGV, Peru’s sales tax.

The two-payment option is one annual membership. It is not a monthly subscription or a two-month membership.

Your membership includes

  • Early access: from the approval of your first payment until 31 December 2026, in the Early Access format.
  • Annual membership: from 1 January to 31 December 2027.
  • Connect: access to the community, curated connections and introductions, and networking spaces.
  • Find opportunities: access to selected opportunities, with search and curation tools.
  • Learn: one course and one masterclass included during 2027, and access to the Nodal library and resources.
  • Make your work visible: an expanded profile and opportunities to share your work, linked to your participation and contributions.
  • Further training: 20% off additional courses and masterclasses during 2027, not combinable with other promotions or special rates. The most favourable rate applies.

The included course and masterclass open from the approval of your first payment, also if you choose to pay in two parts. The activities will run during 2027, subject to the programme and available places. You do not need to complete the second payment to open these benefits; you must keep your membership up to date under its conditions.

Validity and payments

The Founder Membership 2027 offer is open to people who sign up and have their first payment approved by 31 December 2026.

Once the single payment, or both payments of the option you chose, are complete, there are no further charges for that membership cycle. Your access continues until 31 December 2027.

Automatic renewal

Your membership renews on 1 January 2028, unless you ask for it not to renew before that date.

The renewal price will be the regular rate in force for your payment option, with a 5% discount while you keep your Founder status active. The renewal price may differ from the 2027 promotional price.

Before renewing, we will tell you the price that applies, the charge dates and how to ask for it not to renew. Charges are subject to your valid authorization and to the features the payment provider enables.

Before you confirm your purchase you can review the total price, the payment schedule, the benefits and the automatic renewal conditions.

FAQ

Questions about the Founder Membership

How do the two payments work?

You pay the first of two equal payments when you sign up. The second is charged automatically two calendar months after the date your first payment is approved, or on the last day of that month if it has no such day. Once your first payment is approved, NODAL confirms the exact date of the second.

What if an automatic payment fails?

NODAL lets you know, and you keep your benefits during a grace period of ten calendar days from the failed charge. Retries of the same payment do not restart that period or add charges. If the payment is not settled within it, the Founder Membership benefits may be suspended until it is paid.

What currency will I be charged in?

Peruvian soles (S/). All prices include IGV, and Mercado Pago shows the final amount before you confirm.

Will I get a receipt?

Mercado Pago emails you a notice of each payment, and Sistema Urbano issues the matching electronic sales receipt (boleta de venta). If you need an invoice (factura), write to the contact email with your RUC.

What if I paid with a different email?

Write to the contact email with the email you used on Mercado Pago, and the team will link your payment to your NODAL account.

Does the membership renew?

Yes. It renews automatically on 1 January 2028, and every 1 January after that, at the regular rate for your payment option with a 5% discount while you keep your Founder status. Before renewing, NODAL tells you the price and the charge dates. If you do not want it to renew, write to the contact email before 1 January 2028.

Can I get a refund?

Asking for your membership not to renew does not cancel the payments still due for the current annual period, and does not by itself give a right to a refund. Requests for a refund or early termination are assessed under the conditions you accepted and the applicable consumer protection rules; send them to the contact email. None of this excludes a refund or any other right the law gives you.
