Paying on Mercado Pago

How it works

  1. Pay on Mercado Pago with the email of your NODAL account. ()
  2. Within two working days the NODAL team adds you as a Supporter, and the Supporter courses open on your courses page.
  3. Your membership lasts 12 months from your payment.

Pay with

A card or your Mercado Pago balance

Mercado Pago opens in a new tab. NODAL never sees or stores card numbers.

FAQ

Membership questions

How does paying in two installments work?

Mercado Pago shows both payments and their dates before you confirm. If the second one cannot be charged, the NODAL team writes to you before your membership pauses.

What currency will I be charged in?

Peruvian soles (S/). Mercado Pago shows the final amount before you confirm; with a card from another country, your bank converts it.

Will I get a receipt?

Mercado Pago emails you a payment receipt. If you need an invoice, write to the NODAL team.

What if I paid with a different email?

Write to the NODAL team with the email you used on Mercado Pago, and they will link your payment to your NODAL account.

Does the membership renew by itself?

No. It lasts 12 months, and before it ends the NODAL team invites you to renew.
