Why it matters

Urban talent exists across the region. Shared context and a clear next step are harder to find.

Scattered people

Practitioners working on related challenges often cannot see where their experience connects.

Scattered knowledge

Useful evidence and field practice are difficult to carry from one territory to another.

Scattered opportunities

Calls and projects appear in many places, without one verified path to act.

How it works

Start with context. Find a role. Take a verified next step.

Profiles describe real practice and territory; the catalog points to sourced work; member interest creates a private handoff to NODAL staff.
